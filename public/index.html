About Me

Bridging Technology, Design & Community

I'm Diaaeddin Alhussein, CEO of Al-Noor Marketing — a motivated Educational ICT Assistant and Creative/Digital Marketing Software Developer with experience in frontend development, technical support, and community engagement. I actively help students and teachers solve technical problems while managing volunteer projects across Rotterdam.

Currently studying at Albeda College (preparing for final MBO & HBO opleiding) in the Onderwijs-ICT-assistent track, I completed the "Arbeitsmarkt Fit" program at Ithaka Academie. My internship as an Educational ICT Assistant (November 2024 – August 2025) provides hands-on experience in classroom technical support and digital tools implementation.

Through Al-Noor Marketing, I combine my skills in graphic design (Photoshop, Illustrator, Canva), frontend development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript), social media marketing, web development (WordPress), and Google Maps business management. I'm an active volunteer with BuurtBuik, Gelukswandelingen, and youth organizations, plus I mentor SMEs (restaurants, shops) to grow their digital presence.
